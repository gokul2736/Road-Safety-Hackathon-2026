1. The Statistical Blueprint: Why RapidResQ MattersTo impress the CoERS judges, your pitch deck must be grounded in reality. The data shows exactly who is dying and when, which should dictate how your triage bot operates.The Scale of the Crisis: India recorded 422,659 traffic accidents in 2021, and by 2022, fatalities reached 168,491 (Durga et al., 2024).The Vulnerable Demographic: Young adults are disproportionately affected. Individuals aged 18–35 account for 46.7% of all road traffic accident victims, with males making up 53.5% of cases (Adhyapak, n.d.).The Two-Wheeler Epidemic: Powered two-wheeler (PTW) users account for nearly 46% of all road fatalities, primarily due to severe head injuries (Sukumar et al., 2025).The Time Factor: The highest frequency of accidents occurs during peak evening hours, specifically between 6:00 PM and 9:00 PM (Darivemula et al., 2025).Here is a tool to help you visualize and explore this demographic data for your presentation:2. The AI Tool Stack: What to Do and What to AvoidUsing tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and automated Git assistants (like Cursor or GitHub Copilot) will supercharge your workflow, but they can also destroy your project if used poorly.✅ The GOOD (How to use them):Claude 3.5 Sonnet / ChatGPT (GPT-4o): Use these for Architecture & Documentation. Feed them the hackathon rules and ask them to draft your "Software Assumption Document" and outline your 7-slide presentation. They are also excellent for writing the complex Regex or intent-parsing logic for your NLP pipeline.Perplexity AI: Use this strictly for Live Data & Research. Ask Perplexity to "Find the exact GPS coordinates of the top 10 trauma centers near Kuthambakkam, Tamil Nadu, formatted as a JSON array." It will give you the real data you need for your routing logic without you having to manually scrape Google Maps.Cursor / GitHub Copilot: Use these for Boilerplate & Debugging. Let them write the basic Telegram Bot setup, the Haversine distance formula in Python, and your Dockerfile.❌ The BAD (What to avoid):Blind Git Pushing (AutoGPT/Automated Agents): Do not let an AI agent automatically commit and push code to your main GitHub branch. In a 13-day sprint, an AI hallucination can overwrite your working routing logic hours before submission. Always review the diffs manually.Using LLMs for Math/Distance: Do not ask an LLM to calculate the distance between two GPS coordinates in real-time. They are terrible at math and will hallucinate. Use the LLM to write the Python scikit-learn script, but let the Python script do the actual math.Fake Datasets: Do not let ChatGPT "generate a sample dataset of hospitals." The judges will instantly notice if you are routing an ambulance to a fictional hospital. Use real, verified local data.3. Data-Backed Features for RapidResQBased on the statistics above, here are three features you can build into your bot that will make the judges realize you actually did your research:The "Two-Wheeler Protocol" (High Priority): Since 46% of deaths are bikers, add a simple logic gate. If the user input mentions "bike," "motorcycle," or "scooter," the bot should automatically flag the incident as CRITICAL: Potential Head Trauma and instantly route to a facility equipped with a neurology/trauma unit, bypassing smaller clinics.Peak-Hour Traffic Routing: Since most accidents happen between 6 PM and 9 PM, integrate a basic time-check in your Python backend. If an SOS is triggered during these hours, the bot can output a warning: "Peak traffic hours detected. Alerting nearest highway patrol unit to clear lanes for ambulance route." (Even if simulated, this shows massive foresight).Automated Triage Summaries: When a user reports an accident, the bot shouldn't just send a map link back. It should generate a clean, 3-line dispatch summary:RAPIDRESQ DISPATCHType: Two-Wheeler Collision (High Risk)Location: Kuthambakkam Highway (Lat, Lng)Nearest Trauma Center: Saveetha Medical College (3.2 km away)Focus your energy strictly on the logic and the presentation.
